Scaling experiment, paper Section 3.3 (Table 2, Figure 3); wall times of Section 3.5 and of the appendix on exact Gibbs sampling
Input: reference_outputs/fits_long.csv (15600 fits)
Design: f1 with the Fourier dictionary and cubic B-splines (equispaced x); f2 with the Chebyshev dictionary and cubic B-splines
  (Gauss-Chebyshev nodes); n = 1000, 1500, ..., 20000 (39 values), J = floor(n^0.7) from 125 to 1024; 100 data sets per n; one chain per fit.
Complete run (39 n x 100 data sets x 4 pairs): yes
Rates and gammas are least-squares slopes on log n of the log of the mean over the data sets. Values are rounded as in the paper.
