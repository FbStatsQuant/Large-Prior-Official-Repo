\section*{Appendix}

The Appendix is organized as follows. The proofs of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} and Corollary~\ref{cor:anisotropic} follow, together with the formal description of the admissible smoothness ranges. Both proofs have the same layout: the supporting lemmas are stated first, the result is proved from them, the lemmas are proved next, and the conditions on $n$ used along the way are collected at the end. Next come the statements and proofs of the two sparse approximation lemmas invoked in Section~\ref{sec:adaptive-rates}. The Appendix closes with the Gibbs sampler used in Section~\ref{sec-verify}: its updates, its cost for fixed dictionaries, its validation, and timings, summarized in Section~\ref{sec:computation}.
